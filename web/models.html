How to read the metrics

SRC / Spearman rho

Rank agreement between predicted and observed popularity. Higher is better.

MAE

Average absolute error in label units. Lower is better and this is one of the challenge metrics.

RMSE

Square-root mean squared error; larger misses receive more weight.

R²

Variance explained relative to a mean predictor. It can be negative and is not classification accuracy.

Why six variants?

They compare sparse-text linear fitting, loss functions, structured nonlinear models with different capacity, and text/context fusion. This is a controlled project ladder, not a verbatim reproduction of six challenge teams.

Why no accuracy or F1?

The target is continuous regression. Accuracy/F1 require a defined classification label and threshold; no such secondary task is claimed.

Why no test metrics?

Test labels are withheld as -1. We generate predictions for all test rows but do not compare them to placeholders.
